# GUANYU HTML Quality Checklist

## Content and structure

- Facts, metrics, dates, units, sources, and claims are traceable.
- Each page has one narrative job and one primary claim.
- A selected GUANYU page type is recorded for each presentation page.
- No internal planning notes or template names appear in audience-facing content.

## Brand

- Only MiSans and Switzer are used, or substitutions are explicitly disclosed.
- Only black, white, silver, and approved image colors are used.
- Logo is an approved PNG, used once where required, with correct contrast and no distortion.
- No generic blue technology palette, rainbow chart, large gradient, slanted separator, or decorative dashboard noise appears.

## Layout

- Presentation remains 16:9 and aligns to the 12-column grid.
- Text does not overflow, clip, overlap, or wrap unexpectedly.
- Type stays above the role minimum; dense content is split or restructured.
- Header/footer behavior matches the selected page type.

## Interaction and responsive behavior

- Arrow/space navigation and any controls work from the keyboard.
- Focus states are visible and status changes are understandable.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is supported.
- Responsive pages are checked at approximately 320px, 768px, and 1280px widths.

## Final verification

- Render every presentation page and inspect full-size screenshots.
- Check font loading, Logo loading, relative asset paths, console errors, and broken images.
- Record any provisional/planned layout or external asset limitation in the delivery note.
