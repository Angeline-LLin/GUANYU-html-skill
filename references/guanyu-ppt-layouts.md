## Page families

### Foundation

- `COV-01-DARK`: dark text-led cover.
- `COV-02-IMAGE`: image-led cover with image-left/text-right variant.
- `COV-03-EDITORIAL`: editorial cover with image and lower title.
- `NAV-01-AGENDA`: grouped index for 3-6 sections.
- `NAV-02-AGENDA-PROGRESS`: numbered progress index for 5-10 sections.
- `SEC-01-DARK`: restrained dark section divider.
- `SEC-02-NUMBERED`: numbered section divider.
- `SEC-03-IMAGE`: image-led section divider.
- `END-01-CONTACT`: planned; do not claim as approved or fully specified.

### Narrative

- `NAR-01-KEY`: one key statement with an image.
- `NAR-02-TEXT`: standard explanation, approximately 60-120 Chinese characters.
- `NAR-03-QUOTE`: verified quote or declaration.
- `NAR-04-LIST`: 3-5 parallel points.
- `CMP-01-TWO-WAY`: two equal-weight concepts.
- `CMP-03-PRICING`: three-tier proposal, pricing, or entitlement structure.
- `SUM-01-EXECUTIVE`: three-point conclusion and action summary.

### Visual

- `IMG-01-HERO`: planned; do not treat as approved.
- `IMG-02-SPLIT`: text-left/image-right with proof caption.
- `CASE-01-STUDY`: case background, action, result, and evidence.
- `PRD-01-SHOWCASE`: product visual, value, and at most three parameters.
- `IMG-04-GALLERY`: 3-6 related images.

### Structure

- `STR-01-PROCESS`: 3-5 horizontal steps.
- `STR-02-TIMELINE`: 3-6 dated milestones.
- `STR-03-ROADMAP`: 3-5 future stages.
- `STR-04-ORG`: planned; do not treat as approved.
- `STR-05-ARCH`: 3-5 layers or 6-12 nodes.
- `STR-06-PROCESS-VERTICAL`: 4-6 vertical stages.
- `STR-07-ECOSYSTEM`: one core and 4-8 related nodes.

### Evidence

- `DAT-01-KPI`: one main metric with definition, date, unit, and source.
- `DAT-02-CHART`: one chart and one clear conclusion; normally no more than eight categories.
- `DAT-03-TABLE`: 4-8 rows and 3-6 columns.
- `DAT-04-MATRIX`: 3-5 options by 3-6 conditions.
- `DAT-05-KPI-SET`: 3-4 aligned metrics.
- `DAT-06-CHART-COMPARE`: 2 or 4 charts sharing scale, axes, and legend semantics.

## Capacity and selection

- Give each page one narrative job and one primary claim.
- Match page type to the story before optimizing visual novelty.
- Use the calibrated PPT examples for geometry and rhythm.
- For overflow: shorten -> restructure -> split -> change page type.
- The source rules mark many page types as `provisional` and some as `planned`; disclose this status when relevant and never present a planned page as brand-approved.
